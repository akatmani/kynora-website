The brief

Dacy runs a restaurant’s back office in one place: stock, recipe costs, rotas and payroll, food-safety checks, bookings and live analytics. We built the product, from the web dashboard to the apps staff use on shift.

Independent restaurants were running on clipboards, spreadsheets and memory. Real food cost was a month-end surprise, stock counts ate a morning, and rotas, clock-in and payroll lived in three different places.

The brief was one system that connects to the till, does the counting for you, and is simple enough that the team actually uses it on their phones.

What we did

Restaurant operations, run from one system.

  1. 01

    Product and UX

    One dashboard for the whole operation, built around the five jobs that eat a manager’s week, with a before-and-after for each.

  2. 02

    Live stock and recipe costing

    Every sale deducts its own ingredients, so stock, margin per dish and reorder flags stay right without anyone counting.

  3. 03

    Rota, clock-in and payroll

    Build the rota, staff clock in on their phones, hours land in payroll. One place, one number.

  4. 04

    Integrations and apps

    Connections to tills and delivery apps such as Square, Toast, Clover, Uber Eats and Deliveroo, plus native iOS and Android apps for staff.
