The brief

Time Piece is a Birmingham dealer in pre-owned Rolex, Audemars Piguet, Patek Philippe, Richard Mille, Cartier and Omega. We built an online store worthy of the watches, with buying, selling and part-exchange in one place.

A £70,000 watch is not an impulse buy. People want to see the exact piece, know it is genuine, and trust the person selling it before they spend a penny.

Time Piece also buys and part-exchanges, so the site had to work just as hard for sellers: a quick, honest way to get a figure without walking into a shop.
