The brief

Plug-in solar became legal in Great Britain in August 2026, and Solarfy was ready with a complete kit. Our job was to make a brand-new category make sense, and make people trust it enough to buy online.

Most British households had never heard of plug-in solar. The ones who had assumed it meant scaffolding, an installer and a five-figure quote.

Solarfy’s team had already shipped over 10,000 orders in Germany. In Britain they were starting from zero, with a product people did not know was allowed.

What we did

Selling plug-in solar to Britain, from the month it became legal.

  1. 01

    Positioning

    We led with honesty: real savings figures with the working shown, not the inflated numbers the rest of the category uses.

  2. 02

    Launch campaigns

    Paid social built around the three questions everyone asks first: is it legal, what does it save, and how hard is it to set up.

  3. 03

    Content

    Explainers on legality, set-up and savings that answer objections before they become reasons not to buy.

  4. 04

    Email and offers

    A members-only list and seasonal offers, such as Black Friday, that turn interest into orders.
