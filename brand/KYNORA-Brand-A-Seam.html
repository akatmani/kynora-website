01 — The idea

We build the work and the world around it.

KYNORA WORKS is one team for software, marketing, design and culture. The product and the story are made in the same room, so what launches already works and already has people talking.

Technical in substance

Everything we make is engineered properly. Precision is the floor, not the pitch.

Creative in expression

We have a point of view and show it. The work should be recognisable before you read the name.

Cultural in spirit

We make things people bring up in conversation. The brand should feel like a place, not a vendor.

02 — Concept

One letter, two practices.

The build

The stem. Vertical, load-bearing, exact. Software, systems, the thing that has to work.

The story

The beam. An open angle that projects outward like a signal or a megaphone. Marketing, launches, reach.

The seam

The gap between them is constant and deliberate. It is where the two practices meet, and it is what makes the K ours. Read quickly, it is also |<: a cursor and a bracket.

05 — Construction

Drawn, not typed.

011u = stem width. Every letter in the wordmark is built from it.
02Seam = 0.3u. It never closes, never widens.
03Arms meet at the exact vertical centre.
04All terminals are cut flat, top and bottom. No rounded ends.

Always use the master artwork files. The logo is never re-typed or redrawn.

Mostly Ivory. A little red goes a long way.

Display is for headlines only, never paragraphs. Text does the reading. Mono is always small, always capitals, and carries facts: numbers, dates, labels. On Monument: its Ultrabold is a great swap for the display face, but don’t set body copy in Ultralight. Thin extended letters fall apart at reading sizes.
