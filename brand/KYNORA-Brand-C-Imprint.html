01 — The idea

We build the work and the world around it.

KYNORA WORKS is one team for software, marketing, design and culture. The product and the story are made in the same room, so what launches already works and already has people talking.

Technical in substance

Everything we make is engineered properly. Precision is the floor, not the pitch.

Creative in expression

We have a point of view and show it. The work should be recognisable before you read the name.

Cultural in spirit

We make things people bring up in conversation. The brand should feel like a place, not a vendor.

02 — Concept

A studio that publishes its work.

Roman and italic

“Kynora” is set upright: the studio, steady. “Works” is italic: the output, in motion. Two voices, one line.

Publisher, not agency

We release work like a small press releases books. Numbered, credited, dated, collectable.

Quiet confidence

Vermilion is used once, on “Works”. Everything else is ink on paper. Restraint is the flex.

05 — Construction

Set once, then locked.

01Set in Instrument Serif, tracking −1.5%, then outlined. Never retype it.
02The word space is tightened to 70% so the two words read as one name.
03“Works” may be Vermilion or match “Kynora”. Never another colour.
04Monogram Kw: the italic w tucks under the K's arm by 8%.

Always use the master artwork files. The logo is never re-typed or redrawn.

10 — Colour in use

Mostly Ivory. A little red goes a long way.

Ivory 60%Carbon 26%Stone 7%Moss 3%Vermilion 4%

Vermilion is the signal, so it only works if it is rare. One Vermilion moment per screen or page: a button, a logo accent, a highlight. For small text or links on Ivory use Brick: same family, readable.
